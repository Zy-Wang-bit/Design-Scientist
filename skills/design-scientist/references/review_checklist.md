# Review Checklist

Use this before presenting a panel recommendation or scientific conclusion.

## Data Integrity

- Are all primary endpoints from allowlisted standardized tables?
- Are raw CSVs only used in a schema-audit stage?
- Are source rows/cells or standardized row IDs preserved?
- Is `n_synthetic_records` zero for real-evidence claims?

## Evidence Integrity

- Are primary matched, near-matched, descriptive, and model-derived results separated?
- Are measurement types kept separate unless explicitly normalized or stratified?
- Are pH 6.0 and pH 7.4 comparisons matched on variant and condition?
- Are unsupported conclusions listed?

## Design Integrity

- Does the recommendation prioritize feasible performance?
- Are explanatory contrasts tied to champions or high-value decisions?
- Are controls/repeats/QC included when risk justifies them?
- Are genotype/antigen coverage and expression risks flagged?

## Evaluation Integrity

- Are baselines included?
- Is any offline masking evaluation described honestly?
- Are policy weights or batch proportions recorded?
- Are assumptions and limitations visible in the decision report?
